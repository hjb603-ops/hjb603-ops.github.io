About Me

I’m an International Year One Computer Science student at the University of York. Moving to a new country and studying at the same time has pushed me to become more independent and focused.

I’m particularly interested in web development and enjoy building things that are both functional and well presented.

My goal is to keep improving and eventually build software that is practical and useful.

One of the main things I have learned so far is that improvement comes from actually building things, not just studying theory. Every time I work on a project, I notice areas that can be improved, whether that is layout, structure, or how the code is written. This portfolio reflects that process. It is not just a final product, but also a record of how my work has developed over time.

What I Have Improved

This section summarises how my website has developed over time.

At the start, my website was quite basic and mainly focused on structure. As I continued working on it, I made several improvements to both design and usability. For example, I improved the layout by adding clearer sections and better spacing. I also made the navigation more consistent so that users can move around the site more easily.

Another important improvement was adding more detailed content. Instead of just listing projects, I expanded the descriptions to explain what I did, what technologies were used, and what challenges I faced. This made the portfolio more useful from an academic point of view and easier to understand overall.
